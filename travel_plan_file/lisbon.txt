## Things to Do

**Beaches:**
- **Costa da Caparica**: Known for its long sandy beaches and surf culture. [More Info](https://www.geekyexplorer.com/lisbon-beaches)
- **Praia da Conceição**: Located in Cascais, easily accessible by public transport. [More Info](https://www.oliverstravels.com/blog/a-guide-to-lisbon-coast)

**Nightlife:**
- **Lux Frágil**: Iconic club known for electronic music, open until 7 am. [More Info](https://www.reddit.com/r/VisitPortugalGuide/comments/1lxy6sl/touristfriendly_lisbon_nightlife_guide)
- **Bairro Alto**: Vibrant area with bars and street music. [More Info](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7tK7zkTd17Q)

## Events

- **Wonderland Lisboa**: November 25-30, 2026, at Parque Eduardo VII. Portugal’s biggest Christmas market with rides and stalls. [More Info](https://www.tickets-lisbon.com/travel-guide/best-time-to-visit/lisbon-in-november)
- **Lisbon Christmas Illuminations**: November 25, 2026, to January 10, 2027. Seasonal lights along Avenida da Liberdade. [More Info](https://www.tickets-lisbon.com/travel-guide/best-time-to-visit/lisbon-in-november)

## Local Transport

- **Metro**: Lisbon's metro system is efficient and connects the airport to the city center.
- **Buses and Trams**: Extensive network covering most of the city.
- **Taxis and Ride-Sharing**: Available at the airport and throughout the city.

## Recommended Trip Plan

- **Day 1-2**: Arrive in Lisbon, settle into a hotel near the airport, and explore local beaches like Costa da Caparica.
- **Day 3-4**: Enjoy Lisbon's vibrant nightlife in Bairro Alto and Lux Frágil.
- **Day 5-6**: Attend Wonderland Lisboa and explore the Christmas illuminations.
- **Day 7**: Relax and prepare for departure.

This plan provides a mix of relaxation, cultural experiences, and nightlife, tailored to your interests.
